# Northstar Components, Inc. — Management Guidance
## Fiscal Year 2026 Outlook (Prepared: May 15, 2026)

> **Important**: This document contains qualitative forward-looking statements.
> Specific numerical assumptions are the responsibility of the financial analyst
> and must be clearly distinguished from statements made by management.

---

### Revenue

Management expects FY2026 revenue growth to be in the **high single digits**,
driven by continued demand in the industrial automation and precision-parts
segments.

Management has not provided a specific percentage growth figure.

---

### Profitability

Adjusted EBITDA margin is expected to be **approximately 17%** for FY2026.

*Note: Management references "adjusted EBITDA," which excludes certain
non-recurring items. Historical financial statements are presented on a GAAP
basis. Analysts should reconcile these definitions.*

---

### Capital Investment

Capital investment is expected to approximate **4.5% of sales** for FY2026
and the near-term outlook period.

*Note: The term "capital investment" as used by management corresponds to
"purchases of property and equipment" in the company's cash flow statement.*

---

### Operational Commentary

- Productivity improvements from the 2025 restructuring program are expected
  to continue benefiting margins in 2026 and beyond.
- Raw-material inflation remains a risk to margin guidance.
- Management did not provide explicit guidance beyond FY2026.

---

### 2025 Restructuring Charge (Supplemental)

In FY2025, the company recognized a **$9 million restructuring charge**
related to workforce rationalization. Management believes this charge is
non-recurring in nature, though this characterization is not guaranteed.

*Analyst note: The appropriate treatment of this charge (normalize or retain)
must be explicitly documented in the financial model and must not be silently
absorbed.*
