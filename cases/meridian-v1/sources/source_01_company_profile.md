# Meridian Cloud Systems, Inc. — Company Profile

**Company**: Meridian Cloud Systems, Inc. ("Meridian")  
**Headquarters**: San Francisco, California  
**Founded**: 2018  
**Sector**: Enterprise B2B SaaS & Cloud Infrastructure  
**Core Offering**: Hybrid Cloud Observability & Database Virtualization Platform  

---

## 1. Business Overview

Meridian Cloud Systems delivers unified infrastructure observability, real-time application performance monitoring, and distributed database virtualization to Fortune 2000 enterprises.

The company operates a subscription software business model:
- **Subscription Revenue**: Generated from multi-year software licenses, cloud-hosted SaaS subscriptions, and platform access fees (accounting for ~93% of total revenue).
- **Professional Services**: Implementation, customer onboarding, and migration consulting (~7% of revenue, delivered at approximately breakeven).

---

## 2. Key Operational Metrics (As of FY2025 Year-End)

- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: **\$880.0M** at December 31, 2025 (up **+22.2% YoY** from \$720.0M in FY2024).
- **GAAP Revenue**: **\$760.0M** for FY2025 (up **+16.9% YoY** from \$650.0M in FY2024).
- **Customer Base**: Over 2,400 enterprise clients, including 320 clients generating >\$1.0M in ARR.
- **Gross Retention Rate**: 94.0%.
- **Net Dollar Retention Rate (NDR)**: **114.0%**, demonstrating healthy upsell and expansion within existing enterprise accounts.
- **Rule of 40 Performance**: 16.9% GAAP Revenue Growth + 12.3% Adjusted EBITDA Margin = **29.2%** (Management target: reach >40% by FY2028E).
