# Meridian Cloud Systems, Inc. — Management Guidance & Forward Outlook

**Source**: FY2025 Earnings Call & Q1 2026 Strategic Analyst Briefing  
**Executive Presenters**: Elena Vance (Chief Executive Officer), Marcus Thorne (Chief Financial Officer)  

---

## 1. Full-Year 2026E Financial Guidance

- **Revenue Growth Target**:
  > *"For full-year 2026, we anticipate GAAP revenue growth in the range of **approximately 18% to 22%** year-over-year as enterprise customers accelerate migration to hybrid cloud architectures and expand database virtualization workloads."*

- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin Expansion**:
  > *"We expect full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA margin to expand into the **mid-teens, targeting approximately 14.0%**, as sales and marketing efficiency continues to improve while maintaining disciplined gross margins."*

- **Capital Expenditures & Software Development**:
  > *"Total capital investments for 2026 are projected at approximately **5.0% of total revenue**, comprising roughly 1.5% for data center server infrastructure / physical PP&E and 3.5% for capitalized internal-use software development under ASC 350-40."*

- **Stock-Based Compensation Trajectory**:
  > *"We expect stock-based compensation expense to moderate gradually from 12.0% of revenue in 2025 to approximately **11.0% in 2026**, declining toward high-single-digits as a percentage of revenue over the medium term."*

- **Billing Cycles & Working Capital**:
  > *"Our enterprise customer contract structure remains heavily weighted toward annual and multi-year upfront billings, which will continue to generate strong positive operating cash flows through deferred revenue accumulation."*

---

## 2. Medium-Term Financial Framework (2026E–2030E)

Management targets steady growth deceleration toward mature scale:
- Revenue growth transitioning from ~20% in 2026E toward ~8% by 2030E.
- Adjusted EBITDA margins expanding from ~14% in 2026E toward ~26% by 2030E.
- Effective corporate tax rate modeled at **25.0%**.
