## 3. Valuation & Cash Flow Reconciliation Convention

1. **Non-GAAP Earnings**: Adjusted EBITDA is defined as GAAP Operating Income (EBIT) plus D&A plus Stock-Based Compensation ($\text{Adjusted EBITDA} = \text{GAAP EBIT} + \text{D\&A} + \text{SBC}$).
2. **Cash Flow Accounting**: SBC is a non-cash expense. In standard valuation models where cash flows are built from GAAP NOPAT or Adjusted EBITDA, non-cash SBC is recognized in operating cash generation while economic dilution from equity grants is reflected through fully diluted share count (88.0M shares).
