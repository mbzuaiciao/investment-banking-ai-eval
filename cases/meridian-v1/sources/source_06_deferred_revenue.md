# Meridian Cloud Systems, Inc. — Deferred Revenue & Working Capital

**Accounting Standard**: ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers)

Meridian bills enterprise customers annually in advance for software subscriptions. Cash is collected at contract execution, while revenue is recognized ratably over the 12-month to 36-month subscription term.

## 2. Working Capital Cash Flow Dynamics

In B2B SaaS software models, deferred revenue represents cash collected in advance of performance obligations:
- **Operating Liability Inflow**: An increase in deferred revenue ($\Delta\text{DR} > 0$) is a **source of cash** (positive cash flow from operations).
- **Net Operating Working Capital (NWC)**: Because current operating liabilities (deferred revenue) exceed current operating assets (trade accounts receivable and prepaid expenses), Meridian operates with **negative Net Working Capital**:
  - Baseline FY2025 Net Working Capital is modeled at **-5.0% of revenue** (approximately **-\$38.0M**).
  - Forward forecast assumes Net Working Capital remains steady at **-5.0% of revenue**.
  - As revenue expands, NWC becomes more negative, resulting in continuous positive cash contributions ($-\Delta\text{NWC} > 0$).
